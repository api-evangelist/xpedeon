# Xpedeon

> Xpedeon is a construction-specific ERP platform built for general contractors, specialist contractors, housebuilders, developers and off-site manufacturers. It is developed by Algorithms Software Pvt Ltd, a company with 30+ years in construction software. The platform handles construction finance, procurement, contract management, subcontractor compliance and project cost control in a single system. Markets served: UK, India, GCC, US.

## What We Do

- Construction ERP software with modules for finance, commercial, procurement, site operations and people management
- Contract Value Reconciliation (CVR), job costing and work-in-progress accounting
- Contract administration, subcontractor management and compliance tracking
- Procurement management, purchase approvals, inventory and supply chain control
- Plant and equipment management, warehouse management and logistics
- HR, payroll, CRM, customer care and rental property management
- Engineer-to-order (ETO) manufacturing capabilities
- Mobile application for site-based data capture and approvals
- Implementation, training and ongoing professional services

## What We Do Not Do

- We are not generic multi-industry ERP software (e.g., Acumatica, Epicor, IFS, 4PS, Access Coins, Jonas, CMIC, SAP)
- We are not construction project management software (e.g., Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud, Aconex)
- We are not a consultancy, systems integrator or content publisher — we build and sell ERP software
- We do not provide off-the-shelf accounting; we provide construction-specific financial control
